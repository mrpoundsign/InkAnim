Core Capabilities

Built for Precision Animation

✨

IAMS Object Motion Syntax

Animate vector art without duplicating canvases. Draw intuitive motion paths or add compact tags: trajectory slides, 360° spin, scale pulsing, opacity fades, depth reordering, and gradient sweeps.

📈

Multi-Property Motion & Easing

Combine linear, in-out, and custom easing curves across simultaneous translation, rotation, scale pulsing, opacity fading, and 3D depth reordering on any vector object.

🎯

Vector-Scale Rasterization

No blurry upscaling. InkAnim re-rasterizes the underlying vector geometry at your exact chosen export dimensions (up to 4096px) so lines remain laser-sharp.

📐

Flexible Crop & Framing

Cleanly choose between your document's native page boundary or a computed tight drawing crop, keeping your layout intact without tedious artboard tweaking.

⚡

Headless Automation

Includes inkanim-cli, a pure-Go CLI with zero runtime dependencies for batch scripts, CI pipelines, and automated asset conversion.

🔒

Complete Client-Side Privacy

The WebAssembly demo runs 100% inside your browser. Your SVGs, illustrations, and exported GIFs never touch an external server or cloud service.

🛡️

Zero Uploads. Zero Telemetry. 100% Private.

InkAnim WebAssembly runs the complete Go SVG parser and GIF encoder inside your browser's WebGL sandbox. Your artwork remains entirely on your machine.

Twitch Ready

Built-In Emote Validator

📐

Auto-Square Padding

Twitch requires animated emotes to be square (1:1). InkAnim centers your drawing automatically on transparent padding without stretching or distortion.

🎨

Smart Palette & Optimization

Customizable color tiers (32 to 256 colors) and optional Floyd-Steinberg dithering deliver lightweight, clean animated GIFs tailored for online chat.

⏱️

FPS & Duration Checks

Preset controls for standard emote frame rates (10–30 FPS) with live validation against Twitch's 60-frame maximum and 3-second recommended duration.
