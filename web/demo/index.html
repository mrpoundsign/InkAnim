⚠️ Web Performance Notice

⚙️ Single-Threaded WebAssembly Runtime

InkAnim in the browser runs entirely client-side via WebAssembly. Due to browser sandboxing and single-threaded execution (GOMAXPROCS=1), SVG parsing, vector rendering, and GIF quantization are substantially slower than the native desktop version.

🪐 Multi-Frame Animations (Tens of Seconds to Load)

Animations with many frames or advanced features—especially the 60-frame Complex Motion sample—require rasterizing every vector layer frame-by-frame. In WebAssembly, this can take tens of seconds to load, during which the browser tab may become busy or unresponsive.
